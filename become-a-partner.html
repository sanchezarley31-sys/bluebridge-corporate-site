The Program

A Global Commercial Network

The Blue Bridge Partner Program lets independent professionals, agencies, consultancies and companies refer clients, actively sell Blue Bridge products, or develop entire markets and industries — earning recurring, results-based commercial income without ever needing to build the technology themselves.

Recurring Revenue

Earn commission on every client you bring in, for as long as the commercial agreement stays active — not a one-time referral fee.

Real Technology Access

Sell production-grade AI and software products already running for real clients — ARLINOS, BBOS and more — with no development on your end.

Commercial Support

Pitch decks, brochures, demo videos, sales scripts, objection handling and case studies — plus a partner portal to track every deal and payout.

Partnership Levels

Four Ways to Partner With Us

Every level is built for a different kind of commercial relationship. Commission structures and terms are discussed privately once your application is reviewed.

Level 1
Referral Partner

For individuals or companies who introduce Blue Bridge to a potential client. You make the introduction — we handle the rest.

  • ✓
    Simple lead registration
  • ✓
    Commission on every client that converts
  • ✓
    No sales obligations or quotas
Level 2
Sales Partner

For partners who actively sell Blue Bridge products. Run your own pipeline, manage your own clients, earn recurring commission.

  • ✓
    Full deal and pipeline management
  • ✓
    Recurring commission for a defined period
  • ✓
    Visibility into your sales and commissions
Level 3
Strategic Partner

For partners developing an entire market, industry or region. Build your own client portfolio and bring in your own subpartners.

  • ✓
    Manage a portfolio of clients and subpartners
  • ✓
    Special commercial agreements for your market
  • ✓
    Advanced reporting and marketing materials
Level 4
Venture Partner

Reserved for exceptional, strategic contributions — significant investment, a fully developed market, or jointly built technology.

  • ✓
    Evaluated case by case, not automatically granted
  • ✓
    Any equity is governed by a separate, dedicated agreement
  • ✓
    Always scoped to a specific product or subsidiary

Independent Commercial Partner

You operate as an independent business — you manage your own time, your own tax obligations, and your own commercial activity. There is no salary, no fixed schedule and no employment relationship with Blue Bridge; your income is tied entirely to commercial results.

Your IP Stays Yours, Ours Stays Ours

Blue Bridge retains full ownership of its source code, products, brands and technology. Commissions are a contractual economic right — never equity, ownership, or a partnership stake in Blue Bridge.
